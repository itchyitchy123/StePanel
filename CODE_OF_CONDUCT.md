# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Participants should communicate constructively, assume good faith, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed fairly and may result in a warning, removal of content, or exclusion from project spaces.
